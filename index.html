Privacy Policy – Focus Flow

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Focus Flow is a student productivity application designed to help users manage tasks, focus sessions, exams, and achievements.

2. Information Stored Locally

Focus Flow stores the following app information locally on your device:

  • Tasks and task completion status
  • Focus sessions and statistics
  • Exam dates and countdowns
  • Achievements and progress
  • App preferences and settings

Focus Flow does not currently provide user accounts or cloud synchronisation.

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the application.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Focus Flow uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • IP address
  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • App interactions
  • Diagnostic information

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps

4. Notifications

Focus Flow may send notifications for reminders and productivity features. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

5. Data Security

Your productivity information is stored locally on your device. Focus Flow does not currently operate its own cloud account or synchronisation service.

6. Children's Privacy

Focus Flow does not currently provide its own account or cloud service for collecting children's personal information. Third-party advertising services may process device-related information as described in this policy.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or third-party services change. Updates will be published on this page.
